🛡️ What The 3 Safety Zones Mean

Real-world bank scams only happen in about 3 out of every 100 payments. That is why our AI uses a smart 3-step traffic light system instead of a simple yes-or-no guess:

🟢 Under 9% — Green (Safe Purchase)
Normal coffee, groceries, or shopping. Cleared in 0.05 seconds with zero hassle.
🟡 9% to 14.9% — Amber (Needs Phone Code)
Higher amount or fast swipes. Bank texts a 6-digit OTP code to the owner's phone to double check.
🔴 15% and Up — Red (Scam Blocked!)
Obvious hacker bot or stolen card. Card is frozen immediately to save the money.

Real-Time Big Data Stream Processing Architecture
Author: Aman Bhandhari
Author: Vaibhvendra Singh

1. What This Project Does (In Plain English)

This project uses Artificial Intelligence to catch credit and debit card scammers in real time. We tested our system on 590,540 real bank transactions (from the official IEEE-CIS fraud dataset). Because card scams happen in only about 3 out of every 100 payments, the AI evaluates every swipe in less than 0.08 seconds, making sure thieves are caught without disturbing real shoppers.

2. How The Technology Protects Money (Step-by-Step)

  • Learning from Real Bank Records: The AI studied 474,390 historical bank payments to learn the difference between genuine grocery shopping and scam bot attacks.
  • Live Highway Tube (Apache Kafka): Feeds swiped payments straight into the AI with zero lag.
  • Trained Model (PySpark MLlib): Our top model (Gradient-Boosted Trees) scores the danger level in under 0.08 seconds.
  • Plain-English Reasons (XAI): Explains in simple everyday English why a card was approved, challenged with an OTP, or blocked.
