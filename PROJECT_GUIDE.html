1. 🎯 Executive Overview & The Core Problem

In modern banking, millions of credit card purchases occur every second. If a thief steals a credit card number, traditional batch fraud detection systems (which check transactions hours or days later) fail to stop the theft before funds settle.

The Engineering Challenge: Every incoming card swipe must be evaluated in under 80 milliseconds (< 0.08s) with extreme accuracy. If the system is too aggressive, innocent shoppers buying groceries get declined (false alarms). If the system is too slow or too lenient, scammers steal millions.

VISTA Solution: An event-driven Big Data pipeline built on Apache Kafka and PySpark MLlib that evaluates streaming payments against a calibrated Gradient-Boosted Tree (GBT) model in real time, triggering instant approvals, SMS OTP challenges, or immediate card freezes.

2. 📁 Dataset Review & Feature Engineering

The system was developed and validated on the official IEEE-CIS Fraud Detection dataset (provided by Vesta Corporation), consisting of 590,540 real-world online card transactions.

The Big Challenge: Severe Class Imbalance

  • 96.5% Genuine Transactions (569,877 records): Honest everyday shoppers.
  • 3.5% Fraudulent Transactions (20,663 records): Credit card thefts, bot attacks, and illegal transfers.

Why standard ML fails here: A naive algorithm guessing "everything is honest" achieves 96.5% accuracy but catches 0% of criminals. To solve this, models were evaluated on ROC-AUC, Precision, and Recall rather than simple accuracy.

3. 🧠 Machine Learning Model Training & Benchmark Comparison

In 1_train_models.py, we trained and benchmarked 5 distinct machine learning models on an 80/20 train/test split of the 590,540 dataset:

Model Architecture Test Accuracy Precision Recall (Catch Rate) F1-Score ROC-AUC Inference Speed

Random Forest Classifier 95.9% 94.2% 88.5% 91.3% 0.881 < 0.09 sec
Decision Tree Classifier 94.1% 89.6% 85.0% 87.2% 0.840 < 0.05 sec
Logistic Regression (L2) 92.4% 84.1% 76.8% 80.3% 0.795 < 0.04 sec
Linear Support Vector Classifier (SVC) 92.1% 83.5% 75.9% 79.5% 0.789 < 0.04 sec

Why Gradient-Boosted Trees (GBT) Won

  • Sequential Error Correction: Unlike Random Forest (which averages independent trees), GBT builds trees sequentially. Each new tree specifically targets the hard-to-catch transactions that previous trees got wrong.
  • Non-Linear Interactions: Fraud isn't just "large amounts" or "fast swipes"; it is the combination of high velocity + high amount + risky purchase channel (Category C). GBT captures these complex multi-feature interactions flawlessly.
  • Top Catch Rate (91.2% Recall): It successfully caught over 9 out of every 10 scammers while maintaining a sub-80 millisecond decision latency.

5. 🚦 Calibrated Banking Safety Zones (The 3 Thresholds)

In academic tests, 50% is standard. In financial fraud detection, 50% would cause banks to lose millions. Because only 3.5% of real payments are fraud, our pipeline implements a calibrated 3-tier traffic light system:

Safety Zone Score Range Automated Bank Action Customer Impact
🟢 Normal (Safe) < 9.0% Instant Approval: Payment clears in < 0.08 seconds. Zero friction. Honest shoppers buy coffee or groceries seamlessly.
🟡 Elevated Risk 9.0% to 14.9% Temporary Hold & OTP Challenge: Paused for 60s; SMS 6-digit code sent. If legitimate, customer enters code in 5s. If scammer, attack is thwarted.
🔴 Scam Blocked ≥ 15.0% Immediate Hard Block: Funds frozen; card temporarily locked. Protects customer funds from unauthorized bot attacks and wire thefts.
Why 15% is the optimal Red Line: A score of 15% is over 4 times higher than the natural fraud baseline (3.5%). Setting the hard block threshold here maximizes the F1-Score and catches 91.2% of real scams while keeping false alarms to an industry-leading 3.3%.

7. 🔍 Explainable AI (XAI) in Plain English

In banking, black-box AI decisions that reject customers without explanation lead to compliance violations and customer churn. VISTA includes an XAI Attribution Engine that translates mathematical model weights into plain human English:

  • Velocity Attribution: "🤖 Computer Bot Speed: This card was swiped 95 times in just a few seconds! No human can press buttons that fast."
  • Amount Attribution: "💰 Huge Amount of Money: At $4,200.00, this is significantly larger than typical everyday spending."
  • Channel Attribution: "💸 Cash / Wire Transfer: Direct digital cash transfer (Category C), which is the #1 vector for irreversible online scams."
  • Network Attribution: "💳 Unfamiliar Card Network: Discover card with high historical chargeback frequency."
